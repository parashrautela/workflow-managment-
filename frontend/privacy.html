Privacy

Studio Iksha project workspace · Updated 22 October 2026

Google sign-in

Studio Iksha uses your Google account identifier, name and verified email address to sign you in, review access requests and connect your account to an employee profile. Google handles your Google password; Studio Iksha does not receive it. The app does not store the Google ID token received during sign-in.

Sign-in does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar or contacts. Google profile information is used for workspace authentication and access administration.

Workspace access and records

A new team member's first Google sign-in creates a pending request. It does not grant project access. Founders review requests and assign access. Workspace records can include profile details, project roles, tasks, messages and files shared for project work. Access follows the workspace's account and project permissions.

Service providers and storage

Google provides Google authentication. Vercel and Railway provide this deployment's hosting and database services. Project communications can also pass through the studio's Telegram groups and bots. Login and sign-in verification cookies are used to maintain and protect access. Login sessions expire; workspace records remain stored until removed by the studio's administrator.

Questions and requests

Contact parashrautela70@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests to access, correct or remove your profile information. You can also manage your Google connection through your Google Account connections. Removing a Google connection does not itself delete existing workspace records.
